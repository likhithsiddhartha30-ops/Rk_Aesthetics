The Studio

Coaching, compressed into nine systems

Almost every professional I've worked with has the same shape of problem: a job that decides their schedule, a canteen that decides their lunch, and a body that has quietly absorbed a decade of sitting and 10pm dinners.

They didn't need more information. They needed a plan that assumed Thursday would go wrong and still worked anyway, one built around Indian food and an unpredictable calendar rather than against them. Each system here solves one specific problem of that kind.

How these are built

01

One problem each
