Delivery Policy

Everything sold here is digital. Nothing is shipped, and delivery happens the moment payment is confirmed.

Last updated 1 September 2026

What you receive

All products are digital files in PDF format. There is no physical product, and no courier or postal shipment is involved. No shipping charges apply to any order.

When you receive it

StepTiming
Payment confirmed by RazorpayImmediate
Files unlocked on your downloads pageImmediate
Confirmation email with your order numberWithin a few minutes

If a payment is held for verification by your bank, delivery follows as soon as it clears, usually within a few minutes and occasionally up to 24 hours.

How to get your files

  • Sign in and open your downloads page, where everything you own is listed
  • Download links are generated fresh each time and expire after a few minutes, so open them promptly
  • You can return and download again whenever you need to; there is no download limit

What you need

Any device that opens PDF files: a phone, tablet, laptop or desktop. No app or subscription is required.

If something goes wrong

If your payment succeeded but the files have not appeared within 30 minutes, email [Support email] with the payment reference from your bank or from Razorpay. We will confirm the payment and release your files manually, usually the same day.

Delivery area

Because delivery is entirely digital, products are available anywhere with an internet connection. Prices are charged in Indian Rupees regardless of where you buy from.
