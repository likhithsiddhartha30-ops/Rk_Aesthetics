Payment information

We never see or store your card number, UPI ID, CVV or banking credentials. Payment details are collected and processed directly by Razorpay under their own privacy policy. We receive only a payment reference, the amount, and whether the payment succeeded.

How we use it

  • To deliver the products you have bought and keep them available to re-download
  • To provide customer support and process refunds
  • To meet tax, accounting and legal obligations
  • To send marketing emails, only where you have opted in

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for anyone else's advertising.

Cookies and local storage

We use browser local storage to remember your cart and keep you signed in. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Clearing your browser storage will empty your cart and sign you out.

How long we keep it

Account and order records are kept for as long as your account exists, and afterwards for the period Indian tax law requires financial records to be retained. Marketing consent records are kept until you withdraw consent.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct anything inaccurate
  • Ask us to erase your data, subject to records we must keep by law
  • Withdraw marketing consent at any time
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf

Write to [Support email] and we will respond within 30 days. Erasing your account also ends access to files you have purchased.

Security

Passwords are hashed and never stored in readable form. Product files are held in private storage and released only through short-lived links issued after we confirm your purchase. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data.

Children

This site is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change affects you, notify you by email.
