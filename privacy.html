What we collect

We only collect what an order needs. There are no accounts on this site, so we never ask you to create a username or a password.

Payment information

We never see or store your card number, UPI ID, CVV or banking credentials. Payment details are collected and processed directly by Razorpay under their own privacy policy. We receive only a payment reference, the amount, and whether the payment succeeded.

How we use it

  • To deliver the products you have bought and keep them available to re-download
  • To provide customer support and process refunds
  • To meet tax, accounting and legal obligations
  • To send marketing emails, only where you have ticked the box at checkout

We do not sell your personal data, we do not share it for anyone else's advertising, and we do not profile you or make automated decisions about you.

Each is bound to use the data only to provide their service to us, and we name our current web host on request. Where a provider stores data outside India, we rely on their contractual safeguards.

Cookies and local storage

We use no advertising cookies and no cross-site tracking. Your browser's local storage holds three things, all of them on your device only:

  • Your cart
  • Your recent orders, so this browser can reach the downloads for what you bought
  • The checkout details you last entered, so you need not retype them

Clearing your browser storage empties your cart and removes the download shortcuts from this browser. Your purchase is not lost: email us from the address you ordered with and we will restore access.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as Indian tax law requires financial records to be retained, currently eight years from the end of the relevant financial year. Download records are kept for up to 12 months for security purposes. Marketing consent records are kept until you withdraw consent.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct anything inaccurate
  • Ask us to erase your data, subject to records we must keep by law
  • Withdraw marketing consent at any time
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf
  • Complain to the Data Protection Board of India if we have not resolved your concern

Write to ritikkhantwal@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. Erasing your order record also ends your access to the files you bought, since that record is what proves the purchase.

Security

Product files are stored outside the public web root and can never be requested directly. A download link is created only after we have confirmed your payment, is signed so it cannot be altered, and expires about fifteen minutes after it is issued. Traffic to this site is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data we hold.

Children

This site is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change affects you, notify you by email.
