Refund and Cancellation Policy

Seven days, no questions asked. If a product is not right for you, tell us and we will refund it.

Last updated 1 September 2026

The short version

If you are not satisfied with a product, email us within 7 days of purchase and we will refund it in full. You do not have to justify the request.

How to request a refund

  • Email ritikkhantwal@gmail.com from the address you used to buy
  • Include your order number, which appears on your confirmation and on your downloads page
  • You do not need to give a reason, though feedback helps us improve the products

How long it takes

StageTimeline
We acknowledge your requestWithin 2 business days
We approve and submit the refundWithin 3 business days of approval
Money reaches your account5 to 7 business days after we submit it, depending on your bank

Refunds are always returned to the original payment method. We cannot redirect a refund to a different card, account or UPI ID.

What happens to your access

When a refund is processed, access to that product ends and it is removed from your downloads page. You are asked to delete any copies you have already downloaded.

Cancellations

Because delivery is instant, an order cannot be cancelled between payment and delivery. Anything you would cancel is instead handled as a refund under this policy.

Bundles

The Executive Body System is refunded as a single purchase. We cannot refund part of a bundle while leaving the rest active.

When we may decline

We may refuse a refund where:

  • More than 7 days have passed since the purchase
  • We have evidence the files have been redistributed, resold or shared
  • The same buyer has repeatedly bought and refunded the same product

Failed or duplicate payments

If you were charged but received nothing, or were charged twice, contact us and we will refund the full amount. This is not subject to the 7-day limit.
