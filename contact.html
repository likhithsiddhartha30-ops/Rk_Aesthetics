Common questions

Which one should I start with?

Most people take the bundle, because the problems overlap — the sleep, the stress and the canteen plate are usually the same story. If you want one to test first, the Office Lunch Guide is ₹199 and fixes the afternoon crash within a week.

Do I have to give up rice, or cook separately?

No. The Corporate Diet Plan is built around Indian food, canteen menus and a carb-heavy home kitchen. If a plan asks you to cook a different meal from your family, you will quit it — so this one doesn't.

My schedule is genuinely unpredictable. Will a workout plan survive it?

The Corporate Workout Plan runs on rotation rather than a schedule — four sessions done in order whenever you reach the gym. A missed Tuesday just happens on Wednesday, so nothing is ever missed, only delayed.

Is three days a week actually enough?

Three days sustained for a year beats five days abandoned after six weeks. Every muscle gets trained three times a week rather than once, which is better for both strength and muscle.

How do I get the files, and what if it isn't for me?

Everything is an instant PDF download after payment. There is a 7-day refund, no questions asked.

Is this medical advice?

No. These are general information products for healthy adults. If you have an existing condition, are on medication, or are unsure, speak to a doctor before changing your diet or exercise.
